Slack's last standalone annual revenue was **\$902.6 million** for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2021, with **over 156,000 paid customers**. Since Salesforce closed its **\$27.7 billion** acquisition of Slack in July 2021, it no longer reports Slack revenue on its own: Slack sits in a combined revenue line that reached **\$8.88 billion** in Salesforce's fiscal year 2026.

This page lists the latest **official** figure for each Slack statistic, with its source and date. Many "Slack statistics 2025" pages online repeat third-party estimates; when Slack or Salesforce has not published a number, we say so instead of guessing.

## Slack revenue

### Does Salesforce still report Slack revenue?

No. Since the acquisition closed in July 2021, Salesforce reports Slack inside a broader subscription line. In fiscal year 2026 (ended January 31, 2026), that line was renamed **"Agentforce 360 Platform, Slack and Other"**. It also includes the Agentforce platform and, from November 2025, part of Informatica's revenue, so it is **not** Slack revenue.

| Salesforce fiscal year       | "Platform and Other" line, including Slack | Salesforce total revenue |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| FY2026 (ended Jan. 31, 2026) | \$8,882 million                            | \$41,525 million         |
| FY2025 (ended Jan. 31, 2025) | \$7,247 million                            | —                        |

Any "Slack revenue 2024" or "Slack revenue 2025" figure you find online is an estimate, not a number published by Salesforce.

Source: [Salesforce Q4 and fiscal 2026 results (SEC exhibit 99.1)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000056/crm-q4fy26xexhibit991.htm)

## Slack users

### How many daily active users does Slack have?

The last official count is **more than 12 million daily active users** in September 2019, up about 37% year over year. Slack counts a daily active user as someone who created or consumed content in Slack within 24 hours, on a free or paid plan. Slack has not published a newer daily or monthly active user figure since, so recent "Slack DAU 2024" numbers are third-party estimates.

Source: [Slack blog, "Work is fueled by true engagement"](https://slack.com/blog/work-is-fueled-by-true-engagement)

### How many paid customers does Slack have?

At its last report as a public company, Slack had **over 156,000 paid customers** (January 31, 2021), up 42% year over year, including **1,183 paying more than \$100,000 a year**. Those large customers made up about 49% of Slack's fiscal 2021 revenue.

Sources: [Slack fiscal year 2021 results](https://slack.com/blog/news/slack-announces-strong-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2021-results), [Slack 10-K for fiscal 2021](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1764925/000176492521000050/work-20210131.htm)

### How many Fortune 100 companies use Slack?

**77 of the Fortune 100** companies use Slack Connect, the Slack feature for working with other companies, according to Slack in April 2022.

Source: [Slack blog, April 5, 2022](https://slack.com/blog/transformation/fortune-100-rely-slack-connect-build-digital-hq)

### How do people use Slack every day?

According to Slack:

- 12 million users spend more than 90 minutes a day actively working in Slack.
- 5 billion actions (messages, comments, custom apps…) are completed every week on average.
- More than 1 billion of those weekly actions happen on a mobile device.

Source: [Slack blog, "Powering productivity in the workplace"](https://slack.com/blog/productivity/powering-productivity-workplace)

## Slack employees

Slack had **2,545 full-time employees** on January 31, 2021, according to its last annual report. Since the acquisition, Salesforce does not publish a separate Slack headcount.

Source: [Slack 10-K for fiscal 2021](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1764925/000176492521000050/work-20210131.htm)
